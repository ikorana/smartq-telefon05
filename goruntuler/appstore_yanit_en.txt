Thank you for your feedback under Guideline 4 - Design regarding the app not being optimized for iPad. We identified the root cause: the device control popups (detail screens for lamps, relays, blinds, doors, etc.) had a fixed width, which made them appear cramped and disproportionate on the larger iPad screen.

In this version we made the following fixes:

- All device detail popups now autosize based on the screen width; they appear wider and more spacious on iPad, and compact on iPhone.
- Icon, button, and font sizes within the popups now scale proportionally with screen size as well.
- The app now locks orientation based on device type at launch: portrait on iPhone, landscape on iPad — so the interface always opens in the orientation it was designed for.
- We fixed several overflow issues that surfaced in some popups during testing; content scrolls where needed instead of overflowing.

The attached screenshots show the updated iPad interface. Thank you for your review.
